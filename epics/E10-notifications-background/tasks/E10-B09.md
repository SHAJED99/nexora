# E10-B09 · every posted notification is un-tappable and never dismisses itself

## The defect

`E10-T01` §4 scopes tap ROUTING (what Dart does once it knows a tap
happened) out of scope, deferred to `OQ-E10-1` -- but tap DELIVERY (the
event actually reaching Dart at all) is explicitly in scope, and §5
declares `onNotificationTapped` as a real `@FlutterApi()` member. Neither
half of delivery existed:

- `NotificationApiHost.post()` (`NotificationApiHost.kt:107-130`) built
  every notification with no `setContentIntent` and no `setAutoCancel` --
  tapping any of the nine user-facing categories did nothing at all, and
  the notification stayed in the shade until something called
  `cancel(id)` explicitly.
- Nothing in `android/` ever called `eventsApi.onNotificationTapped(...)`.
  `MainActivity` had no `onNewIntent` override and never inspected its own
  launch intent for a notification-tap origin.

The Dart side was never the problem: `NotificationService
.notificationTapped` (`notification_service.dart`) is a complete,
correctly-wired `Stream<int>`, published for exactly this purpose --
simply never fed by anything.

## Severity

**S2** -- a real, user-visible defect across every one of the nine
user-facing notification categories (message, incoming call, connection
request, group event, storage warning). Not S1: there is a complete
workaround (open the app manually; the notification eventually gets
cancelled or replaced by the next event), and no data loss or security
exposure. Not S3: this is the primary interaction affordance of a
notification, missing on all of them, not an edge case.

## Fix direction

Two additive changes, no change to routing/`OQ-E10-1`:
1. `NotificationApiHost.post()`: build a `PendingIntent` to `MainActivity`
   carrying the notification's Pigeon id + category as intent extras,
   attach via `setContentIntent`, and `setAutoCancel(!request.ongoing)`.
2. `MainActivity`: decode those extras (from `onNewIntent` for an
   already-running `singleTop` instance, and from the initial launch
   intent at the end of `configureFlutterEngine` for a cold start via the
   tap alone) and forward to `NotificationApiHost.notifyTapped(id,
   category)`, which pushes `eventsApi.onNotificationTapped(id,
   category)` to Dart -- the exact event `notificationTapped` was always
   waiting for.

## What this fix does NOT do

- Does **not** decide what Dart does with a tap. `notificationTapped`'s
  only consumer today is still none -- `OQ-E10-1` (which destination each
  category's tap should open) is unresolved and stays a human/design
  call, not reopened by this fix.
- Does **not** change `NotificationChannels.kt`, channel creation, or
  permission handling -- those are already correct (confirmed by this
  same reviewer's falsification pass over the API-version branching,
  which found no defect).
- Does **not** touch `ensureReady()`'s own contract -- that is `E10-B10`,
  a separate finding on the same task, filed and fixed independently.
