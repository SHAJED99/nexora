# E06-B06 · ChatView composer dead to touch on Redmi 10 2022 (MIUI)

## 1. How this was found
Human report, live: "I can not send any message to the other device." Asked
a clarifying question (`AskUserQuestion`) — the human confirmed: **"Keyboard/
compose never opens."** Not a send-path failure, not a delivery failure — the
compose surface itself never activates.

Reproduced independently via ADB on the physical Redmi 10 2022 (`e4813f500601`,
MIUI, `21121119SG`/`selene_global`), inside a real chat opened against peer
`B8:DB:38:7C:D4:BF`:

- Tapped the message `TextField` at 6 different coordinates spanning its
  full visible bounds (both loosely and tightly targeted, cross-checked
  against the real screenshot resolution/scale). `dumpsys input_method`
  showed `mInputShown=false` after every single one — the keyboard never
  requested to open.
- Tapped the `+` icon (`_RoundIconButton`, left of the field) and the mic
  button (`_RoundIconButton`, right of the field) — both should fire
  `Get.snackbar('Not available', ...)` per `chat_view.dart`'s own GAP-010
  comment. Neither produced a visible snackbar, at 0.3s or 1s post-tap.
- Ruled out "wrong coordinates" / "ADB can't reach this device": the same
  session's tap on the header back-arrow (`_IconTapTarget`, `onTap: () =>
  Get.back<void>()`) worked immediately and navigated correctly, and
  `logcat`'s `MIUIInput` tag confirmed genuine down/up `MotionEvent`
  delivery to `com.nexora.nexora/MainActivity` for the mic-button tap
  specifically — the touch reaches the app's window; something after that
  point in the composer's specific row never resolves to a tap.
- Ruled out touch-exploration/accessibility interception:
  `touch_exploration_enabled` and `accessibility_enabled` are both `0` on
  this device.
- Ruled out an invisible overlay window stealing input:
  `dumpsys window windows` shows only the expected stack (status bar, nav
  bar, IME, `MainActivity`, launcher, wallpaper) — no unexpected window
  above `MainActivity`.

**Cross-checked on the Pixel 8 Pro (`192.168.0.108:9999`, stock Android)
with the identical build and the identical widget tree**: the same tap
sequence opened the keyboard on the first attempt (`mInputShown=true`),
accepted typed text (`adb shell input text`), and a real send completed
(local echo appended, queued-delivery icon shown). **This bug is
device/MIUI-specific, not a universal defect in this file** — but it fully
blocks sending on at least this one real test device, which is exactly the
UX the human hit.

## 2. What's already ruled out
- Not the previously-documented, separate MIUI/ADB `TextField`-focus quirk
  from `E04-B09`'s own on-device verification (a spurious `KEYCODE_BACK`
  firing on tap, navigating back to Devices) — this session's repro shows a
  **fully inert** tap: no keyboard, no snackbar, no navigation, nothing.
  Different symptom; may or may not share a root cause. Do not assume they
  are the same bug without checking.
- Not an app-wide input freeze — the header's own `InkWell` (back arrow)
  responded instantly in the same test window.
- Not a coordinate-mapping mistake in this investigation — six different
  coordinates across the full composer row, cross-validated against the
  header/mic controls at the same time, all behaved identically.
- Not the message-delivery path (Bluetooth/bonding) — this is upstream of
  that entirely; the compose surface never activates regardless of
  peer/bonding state.

## 2a. A related, separately-tracked finding from the same session
While diagnosing the delivery side (unrelated to this bug, filed against
`E04-B10`): the two physical test devices currently have **zero real OS-level
Bluetooth bond** between them (`Redmi: Bonded devices:` empty), and the
Redmi's own native Bluetooth Settings inquiry — independent of this app
entirely — found a nearby PC but never found the Pixel even at <30cm with
both devices actively discoverable. That is a Bluetooth/radio-layer issue,
wholly separate from this bug, and does not explain or excuse this bug: the
composer must be tappable regardless of any peer's bonding state, since
composing a message has no dependency on delivery succeeding.

## 3. Hypothesis (not yet root-caused — this is the task)
Not diagnosed to a specific line yet. `chat_view.dart`'s `_Composer` (§405-468)
is a plain `TextField` inside `Row`/`Container`/`Expanded`, no `GestureDetector`,
`Listener`, `IgnorePointer`, or `AbsorbPointer` anywhere in its ancestry or
siblings that would explain swallowing all three composer-row controls'
gestures identically. Candidate directions for the assigned agent to
investigate — **not a prescribed fix, verify from scratch**:
- A MIUI-specific `Scaffold`/`SafeArea` bottom-inset or IME-inset
  recalculation issue that shifts the composer's actual hit-testable region
  away from where it is painted, specifically on devices with MIUI's
  gesture/3-button nav quirks.
- A raw Flutter engine/gesture-arena interaction specific to this device's
  Android/MIUI version that this repo hasn't hit before in a bottom-docked
  `Row` of this shape.
- Something MIUI-version-specific in `AndroidManifest.xml`/theme config
  that alters touch dispatch for views anchored to the bottom inset.

## 3a. Root cause — CORRECTED after independent review (see Review round 1)
This section originally overstated what was measured. The corrected reading
below replaces it; nothing here is root-caused — see the reframed status at
the end of this section and `OQ-E06-B06-2`.

Live-instrumented the running Redmi (`e4813f500601`) both cold (`am start`,
matching the original human repro's launch path) and warm (`flutter run -d
e4813f500601` attached, for hot-reload iteration), adding temporary
diagnostic prints to `ChatView`/`_Composer` (removed before commit — never
shipped) that read `MediaQuery`/`PlatformDispatcher.views.first` and the
Composer's own `RenderBox` global position on every frame, cross-checked
against `dumpsys window windows`, `dumpsys input_method`, and `uiautomator
dump`.

**What was actually measured:** on this device, `MediaQuery.padding.bottom`
(what plain `SafeArea` consumes) is exactly `0` — the OS's own 3-button nav
bar is already excluded from the canvas Android hands to Flutter, so
`SafeArea` sees nothing further to guard against. That left the Composer's
controls with only the 8 logical px of the composer container's own
padding as bottom cushion, and nothing else. **This is the whole finding.**
`SafeArea(minimum: EdgeInsets.only(bottom: 16))` raises that floor to 24
logical px, confirmed by the independent reviewer's own `tester.view` probe
(faked to this exact device's `physicalSize`/`devicePixelRatio`): the `+`
button's rect bottom sits at logical `1011.6` pre-fix (8px of cushion above
the canvas) and `995.6` post-fix (24px of cushion) — an exact, provable 16px
lift, and a provable no-op on any device already reporting `padding.bottom
>= 16` (identical rect pre/post-fix at `padding.bottom == 24`, matching the
Pixel-class case).

**The `uiautomator dump` table this session built during the investigation
(§ On-device verification) does NOT show what it was first read as
showing, and that misreading is now corrected.** `_RoundIconButton` is a
`SizedBox(width: 48, height: 48)` — 108 *physical* px on both axes at this
device's `devicePixelRatio` 2.25 — not 23-32px tall. The table's own width
column already proved this (`126 − 18 = 108`) in the same rows whose height
column was misread as 23. The tell, caught on independent review: **every
row, pre- and post-fix, shares the identical bottom coordinate `2191`** —
impossible for a real 36-physical-px translation, which is exactly what the
fix produces. What `uiautomator dump` actually reports is a **clipped**
root: MIUI's accessibility bridge truncates the app's root node at
`[0,0][1080,2191]`, while the real window (confirmed live via `dumpsys
window displays` → `cur=1080x2400 app=1080x2191`, and `dumpsys window
windows`'s `MainActivity` frame `Rect(0,103 - 1080,2294)`) actually occupies
screen y **103…2294** — the bridge's bound is short by exactly this
device's 103px status-bar height. So every node `uiautomator` reports is
truncated at that same 2191 ceiling regardless of its true position; the
pre-/post-fix "height" and "flush against the edge" reading in the original
version of this section was an artifact of that clip, not a measurement of
the composer's real geometry. **The composer's controls were always their
full 48×48 design size and were always fully hit-testable** — the nearest
true physical screen edge (the top of the 106px, 3-button nav bar) sits
~124 physical px away, not zero, and this device's own
`horizontal_edge_suppression_size`/`vertical_edge_suppression_size`
settings are confirmed `-1` (inactive) — so the "MIUI edge-suppression zone"
mechanism proposed in the original version of this section has no
supporting evidence and is withdrawn.

**Honesty about what this session's ADB testing could and couldn't prove**
(this remains the load-bearing caveat, and is unaffected by the correction
above): `adb shell input tap` at the *mathematically correct* composer
coordinates reliably **succeeded** on this device, both cold- and
warm-launched — `mInputShown` flipped `false`→`true`, `+`'s `onTap` fired,
real text was typed into the field — both pre-fix and post-fix. This
session could **not** reproduce the human's exact "fully inert tap"
symptom via synthetic ADB taps at any point, on this build, on this device.

**What this PR actually is, reframed honestly:** a safe, defensive increase
of the composer's bottom cushion from 8 to 24 logical px, verified
mathematically inert on any device already reporting `padding.bottom >=
16` and therefore essentially risk-free to ship — but **not** a
demonstrated fix for the reported symptom, since (a) the proposed mechanism
(edge-flush controls / edge-suppression) is withdrawn per the correction
above, and (b) no before-fails/after-succeeds transition was ever produced.
A third reading this task must not lose sight of: **(c) the reported bug is
real and has an entirely different cause** — e.g. the state-dependent MIUI
`TextField`-focus quirk `§2` already flags from `E04-B09` (a spurious
`KEYCODE_BACK` on tap — a different symptom from this report, but not
positively ruled out as related), or a transient condition present only
when the human hit it. `OQ-E06-B06-2` stays open specifically to hold this
open until a real finger on the physical unit says otherwise; if the
symptom recurs after this merges, reading (c) is where to look next, not
a re-litigation of this fix.

## 3b. Related, disclosed finding — now load-bearing, not a footnote
This is the anomaly F1 (above) turned on: Flutter's own internal metrics
(`PlatformDispatcher.views.first.physicalSize` = `1080×2294`, matching
`dumpsys window`'s `MainActivity` app-bounds `Rect(0,103 - 1080,2294)`)
disagree with what `uiautomator dump`'s accessibility bridge reports as the
app's root content bounds (`[0,0][1080,2191]`, 103px — exactly this
device's status-bar height — short of Flutter's own number), on this
device, at every measurement taken in this session, before and after the
fix. Functional testing (real taps up to physical y 2294, beyond the 2191
the bridge implies) confirms the true touchable region matches Flutter's
own number (2294), not the bridge's — this is a MIUI accessibility-bridge
reporting quirk, not a real second window boundary, and it is the direct
explanation for why this session's own on-device measurement table
initially misread the composer's geometry (§3a). Not actioned as a code
change here — out of this task's scope (`chat_view.dart` only) and not
demonstrated to affect real touch delivery — but any future investigator
reading raw `uiautomator dump` bounds on this device should not trust them
as true screen coordinates without correcting for this clip first.
