# E14-B06 · `FR-VER-008` reconnect re-evaluation is unimplemented

## The defect

`FR-VER-008` requires: on reconnect, the app shall fetch the current version
policy and re-evaluate. `E14-B01`'s fix (launch-time `refresh()` call in
`lib/app/main.dart`) correctly and explicitly left this out of its own scope
— there is no connectivity-change signal wired anywhere in this codebase that
could trigger a re-fetch mid-session. A user who launches the app while
offline (or before an emergency policy update is published), then later
reconnects without relaunching, never sees the updated policy or gets routed
to `/version-update-required` until their next cold start.

## Expected

`FR-VER-008`: reconnect (real network restoration during a live session)
triggers a fresh `VersionPolicyService.refresh()` and re-evaluation via
`EvaluateVersionStateUseCase`, re-routing to the mandatory-update screen if
the result changes to `updateRequired`.

## Actual

No connectivity-change hook exists in `lib/` that could drive this. Launch-
time evaluation (`E14-B01`) is the only trigger.

## Severity

**S3** — a real gap against a named FR, but narrower than `E14-B01`/`B02`:
`FR-VER-010`'s emergency-enforcement lever still works on next launch (most
messaging apps are relaunched at least daily), and this does not regress any
existing protection. Not blocking `E14`'s own `epic_14 -> development` merge
on its own, but should not be silently dropped — `FR-VER-008` must not be
marked satisfied until this lands.

## What this fix does NOT do

- Does NOT change `E14-B01`'s launch-time refresh or its fence.
- Does NOT invent a new connectivity primitive if one already exists
  elsewhere in the mesh/transport stack for a different purpose — check
  `TransportService`/`LinkQualityFeed`/`BackgroundObserver` first for an
  existing reconnect signal before adding a new one.
- Does NOT require polling; a reconnect *event* is sufficient, not a timer.
