# E04-B38 · Out-of-range peers may be undetectable on this stack

## 1. Why this exists as its own task
E04-B36 replaced silence-inference with two platform signals and proved one of
them on hardware (`ACTION_STATE_CHANGED`, EARS-TRANSPORT-8, teardown at
T₀+262 ms). Its §8 check 2 -- peer carried out of range -- was **never run**,
and its `OQ-E04-B36-1` asked whether that case produces any platform signal at
all. At B36's review gate the reviewer correctly refused to approve a task whose
DoD still carried an unrun check and an open blocking question on a requirement
it traces to.

Rather than leave the question owner-less inside a task that has delivered its
other half, it is split out here -- the same move B36 already made when it handed
the Dashboard half to E04-B37 instead of claiming it. `L-process-007`/`-017`:
an obligation with no named owner is an obligation that gets dropped.

## 2. What is already known, and what is not
**Known, from E04-B35's and E04-B36's own live evidence:**
- Turning a peer's adapter OFF is detectable, and B36 handles it.
- With the peer's radio off, **both stacks keep serving buffered frames on the
  stale socket for minutes** (2026-09-16 10:33-10:35): `keepalive received`
  every 10 s from a phone whose radio is off. So "time since last received
  frame" does not advance, and E04-B35's 30 s silence rule is unreachable.
- `ACL disconnect ... closing link` has **never** executed across three live
  runs; the read loop's own EOF path always wins first when the adapter is
  switched off.

**Not known -- this task's actual question:**
- Does going out of range produce `ACTION_ACL_DISCONNECTED` at all, and if so,
  after how long?
- Does the RFCOMM socket ever error, or does it block indefinitely?
- Does the buffered-frame behaviour above also occur out of range, which would
  make silence-based detection unreachable here too?

## 3. What this task does
1. Runs the experiment and records what actually happens, including "nothing,
   until the app restarts" if that is the truth.
2. Only then proposes a mechanism. No fix may be designed before step 1 --
   this bug family has already had two root causes stated ahead of evidence
   (E04-B35's design, E04-B36's first version), and both were wrong.

## 4. What this task does NOT do
- Does not change `handleAdapterDown` / `handleAclDisconnected` (E04-B36 owns
  them and they are proven/disclosed respectively).
- Does not remove E04-B35's keepalive.
- Does not add a protocol-level ack without a separate human decision: that
  changes the wire format and is a rule-3 foundational call.

## 5. Risks
- **It may be genuinely undetectable at this layer.** If neither an ACL event
  nor a socket error arrives, the only remedy is a protocol-level ack, which
  is a larger, separate piece of work. Recording that honestly IS a valid
  outcome of this task.
- **Evidence discipline.** Per `L-process-018`: clear the device buffers and
  start a raw `logcat -v time > <file>` per phone BEFORE the stimulus, keep the
  files, and quote from them. Do not report counters.

## 6. Acceptance criteria
- **EARS-TRANSPORT-10**: WHEN a peer leaves radio range without an adapter
  change, the system SHALL either declare the link dead within a bounded time,
  or this task SHALL record, with quoted log evidence, that no platform signal
  exists to make that possible. (FR-ROUTE-009)

## 7. Definition of Done
- [ ] Live out-of-range run performed, raw captures retained, result quoted
- [ ] OQ-E04-B36-1 answered and closed with that evidence
- [ ] If a mechanism is possible: implemented, tested, reviewed by a different
      model than `executed_by` (rule 5)
- [ ] If not: the limitation documented in E04-B35/B36 and the epic, and any
      follow-up raised as its own task with a named owner
